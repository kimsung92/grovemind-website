About GroveMind关于 GroveMind

Based in Shenzhen, China, GroveMind brings software development and design into the same project, from the first requirements to a working interface.我们是一家位于深圳的科技公司，将设计与软件开发放在同一个项目中推进，从最初的功能需求，逐步落实到可使用的界面。

We begin by discussing how the software will be used and what it needs to do. From there, we agree on the development scope, design and iteration plan. Design, development and maintenance can be arranged around the needs of the project.合作时，先沟通软件的使用场景和功能需求，再确定设计、开发范围与迭代安排。设计、开发及维护的具体内容，按项目需求约定。
