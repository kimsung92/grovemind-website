GroveMind is a technology company based in Shenzhen, China. Our primary focus is software and app development, supported by work in AI applications and product design.GroveMind（深圳市智林星瀚科技有限公司）是一家位于中国深圳的科技公司，以软件与 App 开发为核心，同时开展 AI 应用开发与产品设计。

We approach a project through the people who will use it: what they need to accomplish, where the friction lies, and how a clearer digital experience can help. This perspective guides our work from the initial concept through interface design, development and iteration.我们从使用者出发理解项目：需要完成什么、哪些环节存在阻力，以及怎样通过更清晰的数字体验改善流程。这样的思路贯穿需求梳理、界面设计、开发与迭代。

Alongside software, we explore how AI capabilities and physical product design can support a coherent user experience. Project scope and deliverables are defined around the needs of each collaboration.围绕软件开发，我们也关注 AI 能力与实体产品设计如何共同服务于完整的使用体验，并根据具体合作需求确定项目范围与交付内容。
